Emigrants Make Business

What happens when a person moves to another country and decides to build a company there — without usual connections, market knowledge, or any guarantee of success?

In each episode we break down one real business: startup capital, first clients, mistakes, crises, and decisions that led to growth or forced a fresh start.

No "just believe in yourself" platitudes. Just people, numbers, and the honest cost of entrepreneurship in emigration.
