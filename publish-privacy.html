Privacy Policy

Alsu Labs Publish is a private workspace used by the Alsu Labs team to prepare, schedule and publish podcast content.

Data we process

When an authorised team member connects a social account, the workspace may store account identifiers, channel metadata and access or refresh tokens supplied by the platform. It also stores content uploaded by the team, including captions, media files, schedules and publication status, plus limited technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use data

We use this data only to authenticate authorised users, display connected channels, prepare and schedule content, publish content at the team’s request, retrieve publication status and maintain security and reliability.

Sharing

Content and the minimum required account data are sent to the social platforms selected by the user, such as Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Telegram. Network traffic is routed through Cloudflare. We do not sell personal data or use connected-account data for advertising.

Storage and retention

Application data is stored on infrastructure controlled by Alsu Labs. Connections use HTTPS. Data is retained while the account or content is needed for the workspace and may be removed earlier on request. Platform tokens may also be revoked directly in the relevant social network.

Deletion and contact

To disconnect an account or request deletion of associated workspace data, email alsu.labs@gmail.com. We will verify the request and remove the relevant connection and locally stored data, subject to legal or security retention requirements.

Updates

We may update this policy when the workspace, connected platforms or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
